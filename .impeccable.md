# Design Context

This project is a personal portfolio for a Frontend Developer with a unique background in Geology. It aims to showcase a blend of technical precision and structural depth.

## Users & Purpose
- **Primary Users**: Tech recruiters, engineering managers, and potential collaborators.
- **Context**: Browsing to evaluate technical skill, aesthetic taste, and unique background.
- **Job to be Done**: Quickly understand the developer's expertise and "vibe" as a reliable, detail-oriented engineer.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Precise, Synthetic, Grounded.
- **Tone**: Professional yet technically expressive.
- **Emotional Goals**: Confidence, Curiosity, and Stability.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Technical Monolith.
- **Theme**: Dark Mode by default (High-contrast Slate/Black).
- **Color Palette**: Emerald Green (#10B981) for logic and growth, Cyan Blue (#0EA5E9) for structural stability.
- **Typography**: `Share Tech Mono` for technical precision in headers, `Inter` for clarity in body text.
- **Key Motifs**: Glassmorphism (layers/strata), glow effects (energy/neon), and 4pt grid discipline.

## Design Principles
1. **Structural Integrity**: Maintain a clear vertical and horizontal rhythm that feels engineered.
2. **Technical Contrast**: Pair sharp, rigid typography with fluid, soft animations (like the 'drift' and 'float' effects).
3. **Synthetic Narrative**: Use depth and layering (glassmorphism) to subtly reference "geological strata" within a digital space.
4. **Logic First**: Every animation and layout choice must feel intentional and grounded in the data it presents.
